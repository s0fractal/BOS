## Load-bearing findings

1. **Bootstrap/adoption loop.** The proposed specification used a `decision`
   atom before an authority and adoption contract existed. The kind registry
   also required an authorized decision to expand itself.
2. **Canonical bytes absent.** `revision` and dirty-working-tree digests had no
   normative canonicalization, so content-addressing was not independently
   reproducible.
3. **Relations were not first-class.** Contestable causal edges were embedded
   in source atoms and could not independently carry provenance, time,
   confidence, evidence, or falsifiers.
4. **Normative verbs were decorative.** OBSERVES/CLAIMS/PROPOSES/DECIDES/ACTS
   were not mapped to schema objects or authorization transitions.
5. **Several MUSTs required undefined oracles.** Prose conflict detection,
   global trajectory completeness, semantic query success, and prose-level
   implementation-change detection were not mechanically enforceable.
6. **The specification duplicated `includes`.** Both structural relations and
   vehicle payloads represented the same membership fact.
7. **The core commercial thesis was untyped prose.** It lacked confidence,
   evidence, expiry, and a falsifier.
8. **`read_set` overstated observability.** A system can prove supplied context,
   not which tokens a model internally attended to.
9. **Privacy was missing.** Immutable trajectories can permanently disclose
   sensitive research unless descriptor/payload boundaries are explicit.
10. **External dependencies were unpinned.** Warrant, Sigma, and Trinity were
    named without exact contract/revision assets.

## Requested architectural closure

- explicit external genesis and later Warrant adoption;
- normative canonical atom bytes and clean Git cuts;
- closed structural and semantic predicate registries;
- first-class actors, proposals, relation claims, countervectors, and adoption;
- mechanical/adjudicated/research verification classes;
- public-descriptor/private-payload privacy semantics;
- phase exit criteria with no undefined global oracle.

This review is evidence of a model trajectory. It does not itself adopt the
requested changes.
