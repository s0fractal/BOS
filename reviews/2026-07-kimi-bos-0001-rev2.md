## Verdict

Rev 2 closes most rev-1 countervectors (genesis section, canonical bytes,
`supplied_set`, schema-level dirty-cut ban, verification classes, single
membership surface). The remaining attack surface is one level deeper.

## Findings (detailed in the exact response)

1. **Genesis is authority-by-file-edit** — the bootstrap adoption is an
   unsigned commit claiming a human instruction; the authority actor has no
   cryptographic binding. A model with repo write access can forge genesis.
2. **Revision ↔ adoption timing loop** — the candidate has no `revision`
   field; adding it changes the re-gated bytes. The placeholder ritual is
   undescribed.
3. **Revision algorithm lexical ambiguity** — "single top-level lexical
   revision line" is not scoped to frontmatter; body code fences can contain
   matching lines; LF verification breaks under `core.autocrlf`.
4. **Verb→schema collapse** — `evidence` realizes both OBSERVES and RECORDS;
   the schema cannot tell which verb an evidence atom performs.
5. **`supersedes` is uncontestable** — supersession is the most contestable
   claim, yet it is structural with no confidence.
6. **Symmetric predicates without symmetry semantics** — `conflicts_with`,
   `equivalent_to`; `equivalent_to` also lacks resolution semantics.
7. **Duplicate-ID prohibition ignores multi-repo** — byte-identical mirrors
   across repositories are invalid instead of governed.
8. **Commitment-mode privacy is brute-forceable** — bare SHA-256 without
   nonce/HMAC; correlation across atoms; `retention` vocabulary unenumerated.
9. **Freshness has no teeth** — expired claims may still support decisions;
   "watched source" has no watcher registry.
10. **Lifecycle axis has zero values** — six axes declared, five covered;
    status additions require schema adoption.
11. **BOS-E0001 lives outside the graph** — the central falsification
    experiment is untyped plain Markdown, invisible to V1.
12. **Validator verdicts do not bind their universe** — asymmetric with the
    §15 requirement on views.
13. **§19 rubric: disagreement has no verdict rule**; trajectory independence
    is not mechanically checkable.
14. **Countervector atoms are "mechanical" without fixtures** — verification
    entries are prose, no fixture location convention.

## Priority fixes

1. Genesis authentication (pinned key or external anchor).
2. Revision-placeholder ritual + frontmatter-scoped algorithm.
3. BOS-E0001 as an atom.
4. Contestable `supersedes`; symmetry semantics; `equivalent_to` resolution.
5. Nonce/keyed commitments.
